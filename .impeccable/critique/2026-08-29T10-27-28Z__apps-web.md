**Design Specificity Verdict**

- **LLM assessment:** The product surface is genuinely authored for this product — the person+tension+constraint combinator, the concept map's person→tension→move→proof chain, and the safer/bolder/stranger fork comparison could not be transplanted to a generic SaaS. The voice does real persuasive work. But the storefront undermines it: the hero is a stock template (`hero-6.tsx`) with registry defaults — a fake press list (Forbes/Bloomberg defined but unrendered), a generic `/hero-6-bg.avif` backdrop — sitting on raw `sky-950`/`amber-300` classes that don't match the app's token palette (#1d3440/#ffd35c). The promise is specific; the front door is half mail-order.
- **Deterministic scan:** detect.mjs over `src/app` + `src/components` returned **0 findings (exit 0)** — clean. The critique's real issues are interaction- and architecture-level (state loss, missing affordances, IA) and contrast against a photographic/dynamic hero background — all outside a static markup detector's scope, so no disagreement, just different coverage. No false positives.
- **Visual overlays:** skipped — no browser automation available in this harness.

**Overall Impression**

The product behind login is the real thing: hand-built, voice-true, with an accessibility baseline most production apps lack. The two things dragging it down are both about consequences: the user can build things they can never destroy (no delete/rename), and effort they put in can vanish silently (no draft persistence). The landing page is the third problem — a template front door on an authored house. Biggest opportunity: make the free, client-side Idea Lab the public demo instead of a post-signup feature.

**What's Working**

1. **The Idea Lab is genuinely zero-cost exploration** — seeds composed locally from audiences × frictions × twists × angles with useMemo, no network or AI spend, and the UI says so plainly; the "Use this seed" hand-off pre-fills the brief while telling the user "Every field is yours to change." Free play with a graceful, reversible commitment.
2. **The app-side token system is disciplined and accessible by construction** — global `:focus-visible` outline (2px amber, 3px offset) plus `outline-ring/50` on everything means nothing can ship without visible focus; muted-foreground on background clears 4.5:1; reduced motion honored in hero and workspace; brief form has a proper aria-live region; exploration moves focus to the comparison heading after generation.
3. **Auth flows respect both privacy and standards** — correct autoComplete tokens, aria-pressed password reveal, deliberate opacity on resend (no account enumeration), clear expiry framing, `inputMode="numeric"` with slice sanitation on the code input.

**Priority Issues**

1. **[P0] No project deletion or renaming.** Dashboard rows are whole-row Links with no overflow affordance; the workspace has no rename and no delete anywhere — while the DB schema already cascades (`ON DELETE cascade`). A "persistent workbench" that only accumulates becomes a landfill, and clutter fear suppresses the experimentation forking exists to invite. Fix: rename-in-place on the workspace title, delete in the project header with typed-name confirmation, per-row menu on the dashboard that doesn't fight the row Link.
2. **[P0] Silent draft loss.** BriefForm holds all input in useState — refresh, back-nav, or expired session erases everything, including a hand-edited seed; SectionCard drafts are also state-only and navigating away discards them. The brief is the highest-effort input in the product. Fix: mirror brief-form state to sessionStorage keyed per path; debounced autosave for artifact sections with a "saved / unsaved" status in the existing footer strip.
3. **[P1] The best feature is locked behind an account it doesn't need.** Every landing CTA routes to `/login?next=/app/new` — yet the Idea Lab is 100% client-side and free. For a general-consumer tool with no social proof, the demo is the pitch. Fix: anonymous read-only Idea Lab; make "Use this seed" the signup moment.
4. **[P1] Mobile navigation vanishes; no current-location marking.** The app-shell nav has no active state and is removed on mobile; two header buttons ("New idea"/"New brief") mean the same thing. Fix: active-state styling, persistent mobile nav affordance, one primary create action.
5. **[P1] Landing hero is a template island.** Stock photo backdrop, off-token colors, dead template defaults (nav items, fake press list) in the bundle. Fix: rebuild the hero on the token system and the `.cockpit-grid`/`.glow-field` depth vocabulary; strip registry defaults.

**Persona Red Flags**

- **Sam (accessibility-dependent):** hero-6 mobile menu overlay has no focus trap, no Escape, no focus move — Tab walks through the invisible page behind the fixed overlay; landing nav links at text-white/80 over a photo with only a sky-950/55 scrim (contrast unverifiable); brief-form radio focus ring at 60% opacity on amber-tinted cards is weak; the "Fields answered X/6" progress is `hidden lg:block` so narrow-viewport keyboard users lose progress feedback; landing `text-white/60` and `/55` skirt or fail 4.5:1. (To Sam's credit: sr-only live region, exploration focus management, and the global focus ring are better than most production apps.)
- **Jordan (confused first-timer):** "Find an idea" leads to a signup wall for a computation that runs free in the browser — the promise dies at the first click; "Non-goals" has a dead help icon; "Constraints" placeholder carries the instruction then vanishes; "Generate my brief" doesn't say it will create a saved project; "saved multiverse" / "The multiverse is still folded" / "signal lost" are insider voice; the crucial reassurance ("Nothing is saved until you choose a direction") appears only in the generating state, not beside the cards; verify-email says nothing about which inbox app to open or what to do when the code never arrives beyond a bare Resend — the likeliest drop-off in the funnel.
- **Riley (stress tester):** refresh on /app/new mid-typing loses everything; SectionCard.save() derives the project ID from `window.location.pathname.split("/").pop()` — silently breaks on any route change; Cancel during a section edit discards the draft instantly, no undo.
- **Casey (distracted mobile):** mobile nav removal plus duplicate-meaning header buttons; touch targets hover at the boundary (min-h-10 = 40px choice chips, below the 44px comfort line); workspace's 280px sticky sidebar and three-column concept-map footer collapse into one long scroll with the north star repeated three times before the first editable section.

**Emotional Journey**

- **Peak: the idea lab** — free, local, reversible play; the best emotional beat in the product.
- **High-stakes moment, muted reassurance: account creation → verify email.** After signup the user gets a code field and no "check your inbox / wrong address? fix it here" framing — the vaguest screen sits at the most anxious moment. Show the submitted email inline, offer "sent to the wrong address?" prominently, state what happens after verification.
- **Valley: the multiverse with no exit.** Forking three directions per exploration is delightful until the dashboard fills with forks the user can't delete or rename. Exploration without cleanup converts curiosity into dread.
- **Destructive actions are nearly silent.** The only destructive control is the per-item Trash2 in artifact-section-editor — instant, no confirmation, no undo. Build the confirm/undo habit now, before deletion ships.
- **Good end-of-session note.** "N saved · last touched {date}" and "Projects in motion" is the right return-visit signal — low-pressure, factual, on-voice.

**Minor Observations**

- hero-6 default navItems and trustedBrandsDefault (Forbes, Bloomberg) are dead template weight — a fake-press liability even unrendered.
- Three layers decide easing (ease-spring, the .non-landing-ui override, the global hard-rule block).
- Dashboard greets a zero-project user with "Good to see you, {name}." — premature familiarity; swap on projects.length === 0.
- Milestone min 3 / max 6 enforced via disabled buttons with no explanation of why.
- "Rotate angles" advances deterministically by one — a shuffle icon that never shuffles.
- verify-email resend has no cooldown timer.
- No Suspense skeletons on the force-dynamic dashboard — first paint is a blank shell.

**Questions to Consider**

- Every screen behind login is hand-built and voice-true — why does the first thing a stranger sees come from a template catalog with a stock photo?
- If ideas are private by default, why can a user never destroy one? What does "private" mean in a workspace where nothing can be thrown away?
- The idea lab can explore for free but cannot produce a brief without forcing a seed adoption — is discovery a feature, or a funnel wearing a feature's clothes?
- The 6-actions-per-hour allowance appears only after a project exists — how does a first-timer learn AI has a budget before they've spent attention?
- "The multiverse is still folded" is delightful the third time and cryptic the first — is that phrase serving the user's understanding, or the brand's self-image?
