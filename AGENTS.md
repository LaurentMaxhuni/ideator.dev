# Repository agent instructions

## Product naming

- Use plain, descriptive sentence-case working titles that explain the product, user, or job.
- Two-word Title Case compounds are forbidden. Do not invent names by joining evocative words such as `Signal Garden`, `First Light`, `Pocket Pantry`, or `Trace Garden`.
- Avoid startup-style wordmarks, arbitrary metaphors, fused words, and brand naming during ideation. Naming is not branding.
- Prefer titles such as `Offline pantry count`, `Decision check after meetings`, or `Local trace explorer`.
